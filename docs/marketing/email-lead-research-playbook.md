Purpose: Find businesses that may buy vehicle-wrap and graphics printing from Blue Woods Brands, starting with food truck builders.

1. Research the company website and public business contacts. Confirm it builds trucks or trailers rather than operating a food truck.
2. Record company, location, website, business email, phone, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn when sourced. Save source links and the research date. Never guess addresses or handles.
3. Mark email conflicts, stale contacts, placeholder links and uncertain matches. A company offering wraps does not prove it outsources printing.
4. Ask who handles wrap printing and whether it is done in-house or through a partner. Record the answer and the right contact.
5. Draft a short personal introduction using a real observed detail. Do not invent prices, discounts, materials, turnaround, shipping promises or familiarity.
6. Save the draft for review. Confirm the recipient and get explicit approval for the final message before sending. This workspace does not send messages or create outreach automations.
7. Track researched, qualified, drafted, awaiting approval, contacted, responded, handed off or not a fit. Mark contacted only after a message or call actually occurred. Record follow-up notes; do not repeatedly chase nonresponders.

Starter email:
Subject: Printing for your food truck builds
Hi [name],
I'm with Blue Woods Brands. I came across [company] and wanted to ask: do you print the graphics for your food truck builds in-house, or work with an outside printing partner?
We offer printing for vehicle wraps and graphics. I'd like to see whether we could help with upcoming builds. Who would be the best person to talk with?
Thanks,
[Your name and phone]
