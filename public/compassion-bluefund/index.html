Our story

It Started in Front of a Garage.

Compassion Ministries was born during the COVID pandemic, when Dr. Diana Galloway and her family began serving a few families in need. What started in front of a garage grew into a ministry that has provided hundreds of thousands of pounds of food throughout the community.

Every distribution is about more than groceries. It is about dignity, connection, compassion, and letting people know that someone cares.
