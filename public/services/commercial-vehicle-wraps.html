How much of the vehicle should you cover?

Lettering and logos

Use the doors or rear panels for your company name, phone number, and a short service line. This works well when the existing paint color fits your branding and the message is simple.

Partial wrap

Use larger blocks of color or imagery on selected panels. A cargo van can put the main message on the broad side panels and repeat the phone number on the back for traffic behind it.

Full wrap

Carry a design across most of the exterior for a consistent look from multiple angles. Confirm roof, bumpers, and other coverage in the quote. For a fleet, we can plan a consistent layout and adapt it to each vehicle’s doors, windows, and body lines.

What affects your wrap quote?

Vehicle size, coverage, artwork, and installation affect the price. Existing vinyl, peeling paint, and body damage can change the prep required. Tell us if you’ll handle removal or installation so we can quote the right scope.

Send the year, make, model, photos of both sides and the rear, and your target date. For a trailer, include the panel dimensions and photos showing doors, hinges, and rivets. For multiple vehicles, include the count and models.
