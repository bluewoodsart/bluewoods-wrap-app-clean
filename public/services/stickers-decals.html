Logo stickers and promotional graphics

A recognizable logo sticker can support product packaging, event giveaways, and customer thank-you materials. Keep small designs simple so fine text and narrow details remain readable. If you need several designs, specify the quantity for each one rather than only the total order quantity.

Match the material to the application

Tell us where the graphic will be applied and whether it will be used indoors or outdoors. Smooth packaging, vehicle panels, glass, and textured surfaces can require different materials. Exposure to sunlight, moisture, cleaning products, or abrasion should be discussed before the material is selected.

Size, shape, and cut requirements

Provide the finished width and height and explain the shape you want. Let us know whether you want individual pieces or a sheet arrangement. If your artwork includes a cut outline, send the editable file so we can review it. We will confirm the production setup and available finishing for your order.

Send artwork we can review

Original vector logos are helpful for crisp edges, while photographs need enough resolution for the finished size. Include any required background color, border, exact wording, and the date needed. Proof review is the time to check both the content and the cut shape.

Plan your custom stickers & decals project

Match the sticker to the surface

Packaging, glass, equipment, and outdoor signs present different application needs. Describe the actual surface and whether it is smooth, textured, curved, or exposed to moisture. Tell us if you expect frequent handling or cleaning. Material and adhesive suitability should be reviewed for that use rather than assuming one sticker works everywhere.

Size, cut shape, and artwork

Specify the finished width and height, quantity, and whether you want a rectangle, circle, or custom contour. Fine lettering and small gaps can become hard to reproduce when artwork is reduced. Send original artwork where possible and allow space for the cut edge. We can review the design and confirm the practical shape before production.

Plan consistent packaging and promotions

A small set of matching labels can connect your boxes, bags, handouts, and equipment. Separate the branding that stays the same from details that may change, such as seasonal messages or product versions. Include quantities for each design in the request, and identify any variable information before the artwork is prepared.
