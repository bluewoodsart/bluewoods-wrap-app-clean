A removable option for business branding

Vehicle magnets can suit businesses that want to remove their advertising between jobs or switch vehicles. They need a suitable flat, magnetic metal surface. Aluminum, plastic, deep contours, trim, and some repaired areas may not provide the contact needed for a reliable fit. Check the vehicle surface before ordering.

Design for a quick read

Keep the message focused: business name, main service, and a phone number or website. Large lettering and strong contrast are easier to read than a crowded list of services. A horizontal 24-by-18-inch layout is one option, but the available door area should determine the final size.

Measure before choosing your magnet

Measure the flat area where the magnet will sit, staying clear of handles, moldings, curves, and panel gaps. Send a photo of that area with your measurements. Tell us whether you need one magnet, a matching pair, or multiple sets for a team.

Artwork, QR codes, and care

Send your original logo and exact contact details. If you want a QR code, provide the link and plan for people to scan it while the vehicle is parked. Ask for care instructions for the selected material, including surface preparation, regular removal and cleaning, and storage when not in use.

Frequently asked questions

Will a magnet work on every vehicle?

No. Check that the mounting area is magnetic metal and sufficiently flat. Vehicle construction and body repairs can affect suitability.

Can I order a pair?

Yes. Include the quantity and whether both magnets should use the same artwork.

Can I provide a design I made myself?

Yes. Send the downloaded artwork and original logo files rather than screenshots. We will review the design for production.
