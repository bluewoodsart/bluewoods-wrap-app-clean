Commercial wraps built around your business

A wrap needs to communicate quickly. Start with your business name, the service you provide, and one clear way to contact you. We can work from an existing logo or help organize your artwork into a design that fits the vehicle. Your vehicle photos help us account for doors, windows, handles, and body lines before production.

Full wraps, partial wraps, or lettering?

Full wraps offer broad coverage and room for a large visual statement. Partial wraps combine printed graphics with the existing vehicle color. Lettering and spot graphics put your name and contact information in selected areas. The right choice depends on the vehicle, the condition of its surfaces, your goals, and your budget.

Vans, work trucks, fleets, and trailers

A consistent design makes different vehicles feel like one business. Tell us how many vehicles you have and send the year, make, model, and photos of each. For trailers, include the overall dimensions and photos of both sides, the front, and the rear. Indicate whether you need installation or printed graphics for your own installer.

What affects a vehicle wrap quote?

Coverage area, vehicle shape, material selection, artwork preparation, removal of existing graphics, and installation requirements can all affect the price. Photos and accurate measurements let us review the actual job. Final scope, pricing, and scheduling are confirmed with your representative.

Plan your vehicle wraps & fleet graphics project

Design for a moving audience

A work vehicle has only a moment to introduce your business. Put the business name and primary service ahead of a long list of offerings. Use strong contrast and leave space around the phone number or website. A side panel and a rear door serve different viewing situations, so each should communicate clearly on its own.

Preparing your vehicle and artwork

Send current photos of every surface you want covered, including existing graphics, damaged paint, rust, trim, and aftermarket accessories. The condition of the surface affects what can be installed. Keep original vector logos available when possible; screenshots may need rebuilding before they can be enlarged. We review the planned coverage and artwork before production.

Planning a fleet rollout

For several vehicles, group the request by year, make, model, and body style. A design that fits a small van may need changes for a tall cargo van or a trailer with rivets. Tell us which vehicles must stay in service so scheduling can be discussed around your operations. Keep a record of approved artwork to make future additions more consistent.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my existing logo?

Yes. Send the original logo file if you have it, along with brand colors and any examples you like. We will review the artwork before printing.

Can I request printed graphics without installation?

Tell us that you or your installer will handle installation. Include the measurements and any template requirements so the quote reflects print-only work.

What should I send for a fleet quote?

Include the number of vehicles, each year, make and model, clear photos, and whether the design should match across the fleet.
