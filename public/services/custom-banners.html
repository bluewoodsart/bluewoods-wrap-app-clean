Banners for businesses and celebrations

Use a banner to announce a grand opening, promote a sale, welcome guests, or celebrate a milestone. Business banners usually benefit from a short headline, an offer, and a clear next step. Reunion and birthday banners can put names, dates, photographs, and a personal message at the center of the design.

Choose a size for the space

Measure the available display area and tell us how far away people will be when they read the banner. Share your preferred width and height, including the units. If you are unsure of the size, describe where it will hang and send a photo. Long, narrow layouts need a different design than taller photo banners.

Tell us how your banner will be displayed

Indoor and outdoor locations have different requirements. Let us know whether the banner will hang on a wall, fence, stand, or table, and whether it needs grommets or other finishing. Confirm the requested finishing with your representative rather than assuming every banner comes with the same setup.

Prepare your artwork and deadline

Send logos, original photographs, exact wording, and the date you need the banner. Check the spelling of names, event details, and phone numbers before approving artwork. If you want a QR code, provide the destination link so it can be prepared and tested before printing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a custom banner size?

Yes. Enter your preferred width and height with units. We will review the dimensions and production requirements with you.

Can you include photographs?

Yes. Send the original image file when possible. A screenshot or small social-media download may not have enough detail for a large banner.

Are grommets included?

Tell us the quantity or spacing you need. Your quote should confirm the finishing and any associated cost.
