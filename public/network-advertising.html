CAMPAIGN RESPONSE

GPS documents vehicle activity, not an exact audience count. Visits may include repeat visitors. Inquiries are not confirmed customers. Customer acquisition cost requires advertiser-supplied outcomes and an agreed cost basis.

How does vehicle tracking work?

The pilot calls for a compatible cellular GPS tracker, written owner and driver permission, agreed tracking hours, and clear access and retention limits. Advertiser reporting excludes home addresses and personal travel. Equipment and service costs are confirmed before enrollment.
