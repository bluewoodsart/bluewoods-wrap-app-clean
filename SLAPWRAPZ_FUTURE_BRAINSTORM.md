## Initial Idea: Large Illuminated Cups

Working concept:

- Create illuminated cup-shaped display pieces based on the customer's recognizable cups.
- Explore making each display approximately two feet tall.
- One version might sit inside a building as a branded display, decoration, promotional object, or photo feature.
- Create or incorporate the appropriate logo as part of the display.
- Ask the rental/display fabricator for a consultation.
- Find out what the consultant would charge to help design, fabricate, install, or rent the pieces.
- Determine whether the displays should be permanent fixtures, temporary rentals, event pieces, or prototypes.
